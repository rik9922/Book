## 4. Accessibility & Reduced Motion Parity
- Both platforms honor OS `prefers-reduced-motion` / `accessibilityReduceMotion` and provide an explicit in-UI toggle.
- When reduced motion is active, cover hinge and camera transitions snap cleanly without continuous floating bob animations.
- Both platforms expose a dedicated 1-click toggle to the accessible 2D spread reader (`AccessibleSpreadReader` on Web, `Book2DSpreadFallbackView` on iOS) with >= 44×44 touch targets and WCAG 2.2 AA text contrast (`>= 7.0:1`).
